# Forge Christian Reference (Bible Chapter Map)

## Purpose / About This Tool

This tool exists to complement—not replace—your personal engagement with Scripture. It provides maps, apologetic arguments, and doctrinal insights that give richer context to the passages you are reading, helping you understand God’s Word more clearly and equipping you to defend your faith with greater confidence.

It also places that study on a historical timeline, showing how prophets, kings, and disciples engaged the cultures around them. This includes the intertestamental period—the roughly 400 years of prophetic silence and the events that shaped the world into which Jesus came. Beyond the biblical era, it explores the lives of influential Christian thinkers and martyrs: the struggles they faced, the stands they took, and the consequences they endured for their convictions. Alongside these, it shares inspirational quotes that have offered encouragement in both seasons of struggle and moments of triumph.

---

`forge_christian_reference.html` (the build also writes a `bible_map.html` copy) is a single self-contained, offline HTML file. Pick a book and chapter to see where its events took place: numbered markers, journey routes, estimated dates (traditional vs scholarly), a "world at this time" panel, approximate empire overlays, and a timeline.

Mapped: **all 1,189 chapters of the Bible** (Genesis–Malachi and the whole New Testament).

**Genealogies** (`#genealogies`, or `#genealogies/<person-id>`): searchable people graph (864 people) from Genesis to Jesus with an interactive ancestor/descendant tree, details card (dates, key verse, sources), a path finder (supports several comma-separated 'via' people, e.g. Adam → Nathan, Heli → Jesus), 39 preset scripture genealogies, and 'Show on map' links.

**Disciples** (`#disciples/<id>`) and **Prophets** (`#prophets/<id>`) tabs:
- **Disciples** covers the Twelve (including Judas Iscariot), Matthias, Paul, and James the Lord's brother and Barnabas as "other apostles".
- **Prophets** covers Isaiah–Daniel, the twelve minor prophets and John the Baptist.
- Each person has a card with:
  - names and aliases;
  - claims grouped as life, ministry locations, sufferings and death;
  - a "How he died" box marked Bible, Tradition or Unknown;
  - links to the family tree and to map chapters.
- Every claim is marked either **Bible** (with a KJV ref) or **tradition**. Each tradition claim has a reliability badge (Early, Patristic, Apocryphal/legendary, Late/medieval, Local community, or Rabbinic) and numbered footnotes naming specific sources. A claim with no confident early source is labelled "later tradition; no early source", with no citation.
- A chart across the top shows everyone. In Disciples it shows ministry phases plus ✝ at the approximate death, with a range whisker. In Prophets it shows traditional dates (dark) over scholarly ranges (light), on one axis from 900 BC to AD 60.
- A wide overview map on the right (Spain to India, generated by `build.py` from the same Natural Earth file) plots each person's places. Bible places are solid and traditional places hollow. When zoomed, places outside the view appear as edge pointers. It supports drag, wheel zoom, +/−/fit and the same drag fix.
- Data lives in `tabs_common.py` (places, helpers), `tabs_apostles.py` and `tabs_prophets.py`. `gen_tabs.py` (run by `gen_all.py`) writes `tabs.json`. It checks that every chapter link, genealogy id and place exists, that every Bible ref exists in the KJV, and that every quoted phrase in a Bible claim appears in the cited chapters. The UI is `tabs_ui.js`, injected by `build.py`. Run `verify_tabs.js` to check it.
- **Earlier prophets** (`tabs_early.py`; 39 more cards, 56 on the Prophets tab in total). They come before the writing prophets, in sections:
  - **Patriarchs & Exodus:** Abraham, Aaron, Miriam, Moses, Eldad & Medad, Balaam (non-Israelite).
  - **Judges & early monarchy:** Deborah, the unnamed prophet of Judg 6, the man of God to Eli, Samuel, Gad, Nathan, Heman, Asaph & Jeduthun, Iddo the seer.
  - **Divided kingdom:** Ahijah, Shemaiah, the man of God from Judah, the old prophet of Bethel, Azariah son of Oded, Hanani, Jehu son of Hanani, Obadiah the steward (flagged as not called a prophet), Elijah, Micaiah, Jahaziel, Eliezer son of Dodavah, Elisha, Zechariah son of Jehoiada, Oded, and a card for unnamed prophets / sons of the prophets.
  - **Late Judah / Exile:** Huldah, Urijah.
  - **False prophets:** the prophets of Baal, Zedekiah son of Chenaanah, Hananiah, Ahab & Zedekiah (Jer 29), Noadiah, Shemaiah son of Delaiah.
- On the Prophets chart, section headers collapse when clicked. The "Show:" buttons filter to one section and refit the axis. The axis header stays fixed while the rows scroll. ⇡ marks Elijah's ascension and ✝ marks a recorded death.
- Genealogy changes (`gen_early_gen.py`):
  - New nodes: Gad, Nathan the prophet, Eldad, Medad, Balaam son of Beor, Deborah wife of Lapidoth, Ahijah the Shilonite, Shemaiah, Iddo the seer, Obadiah the steward, Oded, Noadiah, Jeduthun, Zedekiah son of Chenaanah, Ahab son of Kolaiah, Zedekiah son of Maaseiah, Shemaiah son of Delaiah son of Mehetabeel.
  - New preset `prophet_fathers`.
  - Fix: the prophet Eliezer son of Dodavah (`eliezer_d`) had been merged with Moses' son Eliezer (`eliezer_m`).

**Kings** tab (`#kings/<id>`, data in `tabs_kings.py`; 46 cards):
- Coverage:
  - United monarchy: Saul, Ish-bosheth, David, Solomon.
  - Judah: all 20 rulers including Athaliah, Rehoboam through Zedekiah.
  - Israel: all 20 including Zimri and the rival Tibni, Jeroboam I through Hoshea.
  - Postscripts: Gedaliah the governor, and Abimelech (Judg 9) as a note.
- Each card shows aliases, realm, dynasty, reign (Thiele-style years matching the genealogy view, plus alternative dating where notable), length and age at accession (with refs), capital(s), father, and mother (the queen mother for Judah).
- The Bible's verdict is quoted from the KJV and classed **Good / Mixed / Bad / No verdict**. "Mixed" means right but the high places weren't removed, or a later fall (Solomon, Asa, Jehoshaphat, Joash, Amaziah, Uzziah, Jotham, Jehu). Only David, Hezekiah and Josiah are rated Good.
- Cards also cover:
  - Kings vs Chronicles differences.
  - Key events.
  - The prophets active in the reign, linked to their Prophets-tab cards.
  - The end of the reign: natural death, assassination, battle, suicide, exile or execution.
  - Extra-biblical attestation, footnoted.
- The chart is a parallel timeline (scrolls sideways) with lanes for United, Judah, Israel and Other. Bars are coloured by verdict and packed so labels never overlap. Israel has dynasty bands. There are realm and verdict filters that dim the rest, and markers for 931, 722 and 586 BC.
- The map shows capitals, event places and death places.
- `gen_tabs.py` KJV-checks every ref and quoted phrase on this tab too (496 refs in total). Its normaliser ignores apostrophes, hyphens and spacing before punctuation.
- The genealogy gains Tibni (son of Ginath) and Gideon/Jerubbaal → Abimelech.

**Parables** tab (`#parables/<id>`; nav: right after Disciples):
- Covers all **44 parables of Jesus' ministry** across Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John, grouped under five themes: Kingdom of God (Sower, Growth & Value), Grace / Lost & Found / Forgiveness, Stewardship / Money / Humility & Prayer, Watchfulness / Judgment / End of the Age, and Johannine Allegories & Metaphors (Good Shepherd, True Vine).
- Modelled directly on Gospel Harmony (`hview`): filter bar (Theme dropdown, Gospels dropdown including "Shared across multiple Gospels (2+)", "In Matthew, Mark & Luke (3 Gospels)", "Shared in Matthew & Luke", "In/Only in" each Gospel, and live text search across titles, places, summaries, and KJV verses), parallel Gospel table (`#`, `Parable`, `Matthew`, `Mark`, `Luke`, `John`, `Place`), detail card, and setting map (`#parmap`).
- When a parable is shared across multiple Gospels, the detail card displays all parallel **KJV Biblical texts side by side** in columns (`.hpar` / `.hcol`, read at runtime from `JH_API.T2.verses`), along with clickable chapter links to the Map tab, a link to the corresponding Gospel Harmony event, and an explanatory summary comparing the Gospel accounts.
- Files: `parables_content.js` (themes, note, 44 parables) and `parables_ui.js` (UI + `PAR_API`). `build.py` injects both at `/*PARJS*/`. Search indexes all parables (type `par`) via `PAR_API.docs()`.
- Check: `node verify_parables.js` (desktop 1500×950 and iPhone 390×844). Screenshots: `shots/parables_desktop.png`, `shots/parables_iphone.png`.

- Columns: approx. year BC | **Judah** | **Israel** | prophets | 1–2 Kings chapters | 2 Chronicles chapters. Rows run in time order in three periods: divided kingdom (c. 931–841), Jehu's coup to the fall of Samaria (841–722), Judah alone (722–586).

The card adds the KJV verdict quotes, prophets (linked to Prophets cards), chapter links and contemporaries in the other kingdom. King names link to their Kings tab cards, and chapter links open the chapter on the Map tab.

Check it with `node verify_kings_harmony.js` (desktop, iPhone 390, iPad portrait and landscape: all kings, links, events, filters, no overlapping text). **Intertestamental Period** tab (`#intertestamental/<event id>`; nav: right after Kings Harmony): the world between Malachi / Nehemiah (c. 430 BC) and Jesus' birth (c. 4 BC) in five eras — Late Persian; Rise of Greece and Alexander; Ptolemaic and Seleucid rule; Maccabees and Hasmoneans; Rome arrives. Layout reuses Christian Thinkers: event list grouped under era headers (left), era selector + era intro + timeline strip of numbered events + "Daniel's visions" box (traditional interpretation, with the critical dating noted), event card (date, what happened, why it mattered, KJV quotes, scripture links that open the chapter on the Map tab, places, sources, legendary/disputed notes), and a map (wide map, world outline if a place is outside it) with approximate empire extents and labels placed without overlap.
- Files: `intertestamental_content.js` (places, eras with approximate outlines, Daniel table, 52 events) and `intertestamental_ui.js` (UI). `build.py` injects both at `/*ITJS*/`, plus `IT_PERS` (the Persian Empire outline from the Map tab's Persian era in chapters.json). Search indexes the cards (type `it`) via `IT_API.docs()`.

**Doctrine** tab (`#doctrine`, `#doctrine/<moduleId>`, or `#doctrine/<moduleId>/<itemId>`; nav: right after Hermeneutics):
- Core Systematic Theology & Gospel Foundations organized into **4 modules** and **22 theological study cards**:
  1. **The Gospel & The *Ordo Salutis*** (`gospel-ordo`): The Five *Solas* (*Sola Scriptura*, *Sola Gratia*, *Sola Fide*, *Solus Christus*, *Soli Deo Gloria*), an interactive **9-step Order of Salvation (*Ordo Salutis*)** visual flow diagram + 9 detailed doctrinal cards (*Election/Predestination*, *Effectual Calling*, *Regeneration/New Birth*, *Repentance & Saving Faith*, *Justification* with forensic declaration and double imputation, *Adoption*, *Progressive Sanctification*, *Perseverance & Preservation*, *Glorification*), and a steel-manned side-by-side comparison of **Reformed / Calvinist (Doctrines of Grace)** and **Arminian / Traditional Baptist** soteriology anchored by a Shared Orthodox Foundation banner against Pelagianism and works-righteousness.
  2. **The Doctrine of God & The Trinity** (`god-trinity`): God's Attributes featuring an **LSB (Legacy Standard Bible)** precision note on **Yahweh** (יְהוָה, Exod 3:14–15) and **lovingkindness** (*hesed*, חֶסֶד), **Incommunicable Attributes** (*Aseity*, *Immutability*, *Eternity*, *Omnipresence*, *Omniscience*, *Omnipotence & Sovereignty*), **Communicable Attributes** (*Holiness*, *Love*, *Goodness*, *Mercy*, *Grace*, *Faithfulness & Truth*, *Justice & Righteousness*, *Righteous Wrath*), an inline responsive **Shield of the Trinity (`#trinity-shield-svg`)** diagram (`GOD · οὐσία` in the center; `FATHER`, `SON`, `HOLY SPIRIT · ὑποστάσεις` at the vertices; `"IS"` spokes and `"IS NOT"` bars), and a comparison table contrasting Nicene Trinitarianism with **Modalism/Sabellianism**, **Arianism**, **Partialism**, **Adoptionism**, and **Tritheism**.
  3. **Christology & The Atonement** (`christ-atonement`): **The Hypostatic Union** (Chalcedonian Definition AD 451 — truly God and truly man, two natures in one Person), **Christ's Three Offices (*Munus Triplex*)** (**Prophet**, **Priest**, **King**), and **The Five Facets of the Cross** (*Penal Substitution*, *Propitiation*, *Redemption / Ransom*, *Reconciliation*, *Christus Victor*).
  4. **Pneumatology — The Holy Spirit** (`pneumatology`): **Deity & Personhood of the Holy Spirit**, **Saving & Sanctifying Work** (*Regeneration*, *Indwelling & Baptism into One Body*, *Sealing & Guarantee*, *Illumination*, *Fruit of the Spirit*), and a balanced comparison of **Cessationist** and **Continuationist** views on spiritual gifts (1 Cor 12–14, Eph 2:20, Heb 2:3–4, 1 Cor 13:8–12).
- Every proof-text includes both **KJV** and **LSB** texts (and in `forge-christian-reference`, live switching across 3 translations: `KJV`, `BSB`, `LSB`), plus clickable links to Map chapters, Christian Thinkers, Apologetics, and Reliability.
- Files: `doctrine_content.js` and `doctrine_ui.js`, injected by `build.py` at `/*DOCJS*/`. Search indexes all Doctrine cards (type `doc`) via `DOC_API.docs()`. Check with `node verify_doctrine.js`.

**Bible Reading** tab (`#reading`, `#reading/era/<eraId>`, `#reading/day/<1-365>`; nav: first tab after Home, since Nov 1, 2026). A 365-day plan that reads all 1,189 chapters exactly once in the chronological order of events.

- No Scripture text is shown in the plan itself (the Days 1–7 Companion shows the KJV memory verse, Genesis 3:15); quoted phrases in notes and the Companion are checked against `data/kjv.json`.
- Files: `reading_content.js` (generated by `/tmp/read/assemble.py` from `common.py`, `ot.py`, `nt.py`, `quotes.py`, which validates coverage) and `reading_ui.js`, injected by `build.py` at `/*READJS*/`. Search indexes days, eras and the overview (type `read`, 📅) via `READ_API.docs()`. Check with `node verify_reading.js`.

## Rebuild (single entry point)
```bash
cd /workspace/bible-map
python3 gen_all.py            # writes chapters.json + genealogy.json; verifies every ref against the KJV
.venv/bin/python build.py       # injects map geometry + chapters.json into template.html -> bible_map.html
node verify_all.js              # Playwright + system Chrome: every chapter renders, no console errors, screenshots
node verify_samuel.js && node verify_kings.js   # per-book layer checks + shots/
node verify_genealogy.js        # genealogy UI: search, Adam→Jesus via Matthew and Luke, all presets, map link
```
If `.venv` is missing: `python3 -m venv .venv && .venv/bin/pip install pyshp`.
`gen_all.py` exits non-zero if any reference fails (bad verse range, or the keyword isn't in the cited KJV verses).

## Data model (`chapters.json`)
- `eras[id]`: `name`, `label`, `chronologyNote`, `timeline:[fromYear,toYear]` (negative = BC), `world:[{from,to,title,desc,region,power?}]`, `powers:[{name,color,on,note,poly:[[lon,lat],…],line?}]`, `cities:[[name,lon,lat]]`.
- `chapters["Book N"]`: `title`, `summary`, `era`, `dates.trad{label,year,basis}`, `dates.scholarly{label,from,to,basis}`, `events:[{ref,place,modern,lat,lon,certainty,note,desc,date}]` (lat null = no marker), `route:[[event numbers],…]`, optional `mapNote`, `areas:[{name,color,poly,note}]`.

## Adding a book
Create `gen_<book>.py` (or extend `gen_all.py`): `import gen_genesis as g`, set `g.BOOK="Joshua"`, add places to `P`, call `ch(n, title, summary, era, dates(...), [ev(ref, placeKey, desc, kjvKeyword, **overrides)], route=[[…]])`. Add the book to `ORDER` and to the `BI` list in `gen_genesis._verify` (KJV book index), then rebuild.

## Conventions
- Traditional dates: 1 Kgs 6:1 → Exodus 1446 BC, with Masoretic genealogies (Abram born 2166 BC; Ussher shown for Gen 1–10). Always paired with a scholarly range and hedges.
- Certainty values: secure, probable, traditional, uncertain, region, implied, route.
- Empire polygons are hand-drawn approximations, clipped to land.

## Genealogy data model (`genealogy.json`)
- `people[id]`: `name`, `aliases[]` (KJV and other spellings), optional `dates` (hedged), `note`, `verse`, `map` (chapter key for "Show on map").
- `rels[]`: `{t, a, b, src[], note?, hist?}`; `t` = `p` (a parent of b), `legal` (legal/adoptive father), `anc` (ancestor, skipped generations), `sp` (spouse), `sib`, `kin`, `succ` (a succeeded by b). Duplicate links are merged with their sources combined.
- `presets[]`: `{id, title, ref, line[], note}`.
- Path finding: descendant-direction search first (so 'via Nathan, Heli' stays on Luke's line), then reverse, then undirected (spouses/siblings; successions optional).

## Label collision avoidance
- `template.html` → `layoutLabels()` (requestAnimationFrame-debounced via `scheduleLabels()`; called from every `setVB` (zoom, pan, reset, chapter change), legend/overlay toggles and window resize). Works in screen space using `getBBox` + `getScreenCTM`, so on-screen text sizes stay constant.
- Priority: marker circles → marker labels (event order) → city labels → Levitical symbols → tribe labels → empire labels → water/river labels → region labels.
- Marker circles that would overlap are nudged onto rings around their true point, with a thin leader line back to it. Routes still run to the true coordinates. Circles stay clickable.
- Marker labels try 14 positions: right, left, above, below, the four diagonals, then further out. Other labels try small shifts. A label that can't be placed gets `.lhide` (opacity 0) and shows on hover. A marker's label also shows when you hover the marker, and the popup always names the place.
- All map text has a cream halo (`paint-order: stroke`).
- `node verify_labels.js [file] [--max N] [--shots prefix "Key,Key"]`: counts pairwise-overlapping visible text boxes in every chapter, at the default view and zoomed 2x. Before: 9,507 overlapping pairs (5,549 default + 3,958 zoomed; 290 chapters affected). After: 0. Screenshots are `shots/labels_before_*.png` and `shots/labels_after_*.png`.

## Search
- `search_ui.js` (injected at `/*SEARCHJS*/`). Header box, always visible on desktop/iPad; on iPhone portrait and phone landscape it's a 🔍 icon that expands. The input is 16px. Keys: `/` focuses, ↑/↓ move through the autocomplete, Enter opens the highlighted item (or all results), Esc closes.
